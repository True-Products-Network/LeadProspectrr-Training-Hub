# 🏆 World Cup 2026 Planner & Predictor

A comprehensive, interactive web application to track and predict the FIFA World Cup 2026 tournament.

## Features

### 📊 Group Stage Tracker
- All 12 groups (A-L) with 4 teams each
- Enter predictions for all 36 group matches
- Automatic standings calculation (points, goals, goal difference)
- Real-time table updates as you enter scores

### 🏆 Knockout Stage
- Round of 32 (new for 2026!)
- Round of 16
- Quarter Finals
- Semi Finals
- Final

### 📈 Personal Stats Dashboard
- Matches predicted
- Total goals predicted
- Correct scores tracking
- Correct results tracking

### 🌍 Tournament Information
- Host cities across USA, Canada, and Mexico
- Tournament format details
- 48-team expansion information
- Defending champions (Argentina)

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Click on the **Group Stage** tab
3. Enter your predicted scores for each match
4. Watch the standings update automatically
5. Check your stats in the **My Stats** tab
6. Follow along when the real tournament starts!

## Tournament Details

- **Dates:** June 11 - July 19, 2026
- **Teams:** 48 (expanded from 32)
- **Matches:** 104
- **Host Nations:** USA, Canada, Mexico
- **Host Cities:** 16 cities across 3 countries

## Technical

- Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- No external dependencies
- Local storage saves your predictions
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop

Team placements in groups are illustrative. The actual draw will determine final group compositions.
