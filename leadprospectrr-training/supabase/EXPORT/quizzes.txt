MODULE 21 QUIZZES
================

Question 1: What are the three opportunities from every completed appointment?
  A. Email, SMS, and phone call
  B. Rebooking, referral, and next service ✓ CORRECT
  C. Marketing, sales, and support
  D. Discovery, proposal, and closing

Explanation: Every completed appointment creates three potential opportunities: a repeat booking from the same customer, a referral to a new prospect, or the purchase of a related service.

---

Question 2: Are these three outcomes guaranteed after every appointment?
  A. Yes, for appointments longer than 30 minutes
  B. Only for existing customers, not new prospects
  C. Yes, if the follow-up sequence is built correctly
  D. No, they are opportunities to measure, not guaranteed results ✓ CORRECT

Explanation: These are opportunities, not guarantees. The customer may rebook, refer, or buy — or they may do none of these. A good follow-up sequence increases likelihood but cannot force outcomes.

---

Question 3: Why is follow-up more effective than waiting for the customer to reach out?
  A. Waiting never results in repeat business
  B. It is always more expensive to acquire new customers
  C. Customers often intend to rebook or refer but get distracted ✓ CORRECT
  D. Follow-up is required by law in most industries

Explanation: Many satisfied customers genuinely intend to rebook or refer but become distracted by other priorities. Timely follow-up captures these intentions while the positive experience is still fresh.

---

Question 4: When should the post-appointment follow-up workflow trigger?
  A. One day before the scheduled appointment
  B. After the appointment is confirmed as completed or showed ✓ CORRECT
  C. Only if the customer pays an invoice
  D. As soon as the appointment is booked

Explanation: The trigger must fire after the appointment is confirmed as completed, not when it is booked. Starting at booking wastes effort if the appointment is cancelled or no-showed.

---

Question 5: Why should you filter the trigger by calendar?
  A. To avoid contacting the wrong person at the wrong stage ✓ CORRECT
  B. To reduce the number of workflows in your account
  C. Calendar filtering is optional and not recommended
  D. To automatically cancel conflicting appointments

Explanation: Filtering by calendar ensures the follow-up sequence only runs for the specific appointment type you intended. Without this filter, a discovery call follow-up might fire after a support meeting.

---

Question 6: What is the primary purpose of the thank-you message?
  A. To send a promotional offer
  B. To confirm value delivered and build goodwill for later follow-up ✓ CORRECT
  C. To immediately ask for a referral
  D. To request payment for the appointment

Explanation: The thank-you message confirms the value of the appointment, reinforces what was discussed, and builds goodwill. This sets a positive tone that makes later rebooking, referral, and offer messages feel natural.

---

Question 7: When should the thank-you message be sent?
  A. One week after the appointment
  B. Only if the customer does not rebook within 48 hours
  C. Within a few hours of the completed appointment ✓ CORRECT
  D. Before the appointment as a reminder

Explanation: The thank-you should arrive within a few hours while the appointment is still fresh. Delay too long and it feels like an afterthought rather than a genuine expression of appreciation.

---

Question 8: Which channel is most commonly used for thank-you messages?
  A. Email, with SMS as an alternative when consent exists ✓ CORRECT
  B. Phone call within 30 minutes
  C. Social media direct message
  D. Postal mail

Explanation: Email is the standard channel for thank-you messages because it allows for longer content, attachments, and links while feeling less intrusive than SMS. SMS can work if the customer has explicitly consented to text messages.

---

Question 9: Why is rebooking typically easier than acquiring a new customer?
  A. It requires no marketing budget
  B. Rebooking is always free for the customer
  C. The customer already knows, likes, and trusts you ✓ CORRECT
  D. New customers are not valuable

Explanation: Rebooking an existing customer is easier because they already have a relationship with you. They understand your value and have experienced your service firsthand, removing the trust-building phase required with new prospects.

---

Question 10: How long should you wait after the thank-you message before sending the rebooking invitation?
  A. Immediately after the thank-you
  B. Approximately one day ✓ CORRECT
  C. One week
  D. Only if they do not rebook on their own

Explanation: Wait approximately one day after the thank-you message. This gives the customer breathing room without letting too much time pass. Immediate rebooking feels pushy; waiting too long lets the positive experience fade.

---

Question 11: What should you include in the rebooking message?
  A. One clear calendar link and the value of the next session ✓ CORRECT
  B. Multiple calendar links for flexibility
  C. A discount code for first-time customers
  D. A request for payment upfront

Explanation: Include one clear calendar link to remove friction, and explain the value of the next session to give the customer a reason to return. Multiple links create decision paralysis; a single, direct path works best.

---

Question 12: When is the best time to ask for a referral?
  A. After a positive experience when the customer is satisfied ✓ CORRECT
  B. In every message you send
  C. Only after the customer has booked three times
  D. Immediately at the start of the appointment

Explanation: Only ask for referrals after a positive experience. If the appointment did not go well, asking for introductions feels tone-deaf. The referral request should come when the customer is most satisfied with your service.

---

Question 13: How many referral requests should you include in your follow-up sequence?
  A. One in every message for maximum results
  B. None — referrals should only happen organically
  C. One well-timed request is enough ✓ CORRECT
  D. At least three to ensure they see it

Explanation: One well-timed referral request is enough. Repeatedly asking for introductions feels desperate and can damage the customer relationship. A single, respectful ask at the right moment is far more effective.

---

Question 14: What should the next-service offer feel like to the customer?
  A. A condition for receiving future appointments
  B. An invitation to continue making progress on their goals ✓ CORRECT
  C. A limited-time discount they cannot refuse
  D. A mandatory upgrade they must purchase

Explanation: The next-service offer should feel like an invitation to continue progress, not a demand. When the service is genuinely relevant and presented respectfully, customers appreciate the suggestion rather than feeling pressured.

---

Question 15: How long should you wait after the referral request before sending the next-offer message?
  A. Only if they do not respond to the referral request
  B. One week
  C. Immediately after the referral request
  D. Approximately two days ✓ CORRECT

Explanation: Wait approximately two days after the referral request. This spaces out your messages and gives each one room to breathe. Sending messages too close together feels overwhelming and reduces engagement.

---

Question 16: Why are stop conditions essential in a follow-up workflow?
  A. They reduce the total number of messages sent
  B. They prevent irrelevant messages after the customer takes action ✓ CORRECT
  C. They are required by law in all countries
  D. They make the workflow run faster

Explanation: Stop conditions prevent redundant or irrelevant messages. If a customer already rebooked, they should not receive another rebooking invitation. Stop conditions respect customer actions and protect the relationship.

---

Question 17: What should you do when a customer opts out or unsubscribes?
  A. Immediately stop all promotional messages and honour their preferences ✓ CORRECT
  B. Continue sending messages on a different channel
  C. Send one final message asking why they unsubscribed
  D. Add them to a different workflow instead

Explanation: When a customer opts out, immediately stop all promotional messages and honour their preferences without exception. Continuing to message someone who unsubscribed damages trust and may violate regulations.

---

Question 18: Why should you test the workflow with a sample contact before going live?
  A. Sample contacts are required by GoHighLevel
  B. Testing is only needed for complex workflows
  C. Testing is a waste of time for simple sequences
  D. To verify triggers, timing, content, links, and stop conditions ✓ CORRECT

Explanation: Testing with a sample contact lets you experience the sequence as a customer would. You can verify timing, check content and personalization, test all links, and confirm stop conditions work before real contacts enter the workflow.
