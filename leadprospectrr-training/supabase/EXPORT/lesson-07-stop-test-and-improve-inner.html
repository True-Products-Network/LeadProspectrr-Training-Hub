Learning Goal

Set safeguards to avoid irrelevant messages, test the complete workflow with a sample contact, and establish a review process to track real results over time.

Why Stop Conditions Matter

Avoid Irrelevant Messages

Without stop conditions, your workflow might send rebooking invitations to customers who already rebooked, or referral requests to customers who already referred someone.

Respect Customer Actions

If a customer takes the action you requested, they should not receive further messages asking for the same thing. Stop conditions prevent this redundancy.

Protect the Relationship

Sending messages that do not apply to the customer's current situation feels careless. Stop conditions show you are paying attention to their actions.

Essential Stop Conditions

Reply Stop

If the customer replies to any message, pause or stop the sequence. A reply indicates engagement and may require a human response rather than automated follow-up.

Rebooking Stop

If the customer books another appointment, stop the rebooking invitation and adjust remaining messages accordingly.

Goal Completion Stop

If the customer completes the goal of the sequence (rebooks, refers, or purchases), stop or transition to a different nurture sequence.

Opt-Out Stop

If the customer unsubscribes or opts out, immediately stop all promotional messages. Honour their preferences without exception.

Testing with a Sample Contact

Create a Test Contact

Use a test contact with your own email or a dedicated test address. This lets you experience the sequence exactly as a real customer would.

Verify Timing and Content

Check that messages arrive at the right intervals, contain the correct personalization, and include working links. Look for formatting issues or broken merge fields.

Test All Links

Click every link in every message. Verify calendar links work, landing pages load, and unsubscribe options function correctly.

Check Stop Conditions

Trigger each stop condition and verify the sequence behaves as expected. Test replies, rebookings, and opt-outs to ensure they halt the workflow.

Key Point

A workflow without stop conditions is a liability. It wastes your credibility and annoys customers who already took action. Testing with a sample contact is not optional — it is essential. Check timing, content, links, and every stop condition before going live. Then set a 30-day review to measure real results and refine your approach.
