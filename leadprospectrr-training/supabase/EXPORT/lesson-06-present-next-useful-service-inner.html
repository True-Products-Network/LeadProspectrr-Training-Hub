Learning Goal

Offer a relevant service without pressuring the customer by choosing an appropriate next offering based on their goals and presenting it with clear value.

Choosing the Right Next Service

Build on the Current Relationship

The next service should be a natural extension of what you just delivered. It should feel like the logical next step, not a random upsell.

Match Their Goals

Choose a package, workshop, retainer, or related service based on the customer's stated goals. The better the fit, the more likely they are to be interested.

Avoid Pressure

Present the service as an option, not a requirement. The customer should feel invited, not pushed. Pressure destroys trust and reduces the chance of any outcome.

Crafting the Next-Offer Message

Connect to Their Goals

Reference the goals they shared in the appointment. Show how this service directly addresses those goals and builds on what you already discussed.

Provide Clear Next Steps

Include a direct link to learn more, book a consultation, or purchase. Make the path from interest to action as simple as possible.

Add a Two-Day Wait

Send the next-offer message approximately two days after the referral request. This spaces out your messages and gives each one room to breathe.

Examples by Business Type

Speaker

After a keynote planning call, offer a workshop or half-day training programme that complements the keynote.

Coach

After a discovery session, offer a coaching package that includes multiple sessions and email support between calls.

Consultant

After an assessment, offer implementation support or a roadmap project that turns your recommendations into action.

Key Point

The next-service offer is not about squeezing more money from a customer — it is about continuing to help them achieve their goals. When the service is genuinely relevant and presented as an invitation rather than a demand, customers appreciate the suggestion. Match the offer to their goals, make it easy to act, and accept their decision gracefully.
