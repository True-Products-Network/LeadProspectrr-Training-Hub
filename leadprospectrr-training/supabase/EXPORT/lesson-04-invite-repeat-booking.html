Invite a Repeat Booking

Learning Goal

Create a rebooking invitation that makes scheduling the next appointment straightforward by providing a clear calendar link and explaining the value of the next session.

Why Rebooking Matters

Easier Than New Acquisition

Rebooking an existing customer is significantly easier and more cost-effective than finding a new prospect. They already know, like, and trust you.

Recurring Revenue

Repeat bookings create predictable revenue. A customer who books regularly is worth far more over time than a one-time client.

Deepening the Relationship

Each additional appointment strengthens the customer relationship and increases the likelihood of referrals and additional service purchases.

Crafting the Rebooking Message

Explain the Value of the Next Session

Do not just ask them to book again. Explain what the next session will cover and why it matters for their goals. Give them a reason to return.

Provide One Clear Calendar Link

Include a direct link to your booking calendar. The easier you make it to book, the more likely they are to do it. Remove all friction.

Add a One-Day Wait

Wait approximately one day after the thank-you message before sending the rebooking invitation. This gives the customer breathing room without letting too much time pass.

Calendar Setup Tips

Find Your Calendar Settings

Navigate to Settings > Calendars (or Calendars > Calendar Settings, depending on your account layout). Ensure your calendar is configured correctly.

Test the Booking Link

Click your own calendar link and complete a test booking. Verify the confirmation email, reminder settings, and that the appointment appears correctly.

Avoid Multiple Links

Offering multiple calendars or booking options creates decision paralysis. Provide one clear, direct path to book the specific next appointment.

Key Point

The rebooking invitation is not a demand — it is an invitation to continue progress. Make it easy by providing one clear calendar link, explain the value of the next session, and time it about a day after your thank-you message. The goal is to remove all friction between the customer's intention and the booking confirmation.

Action Steps — Ask for Introductions

1

Identify the Natural Next Appointment

For your chosen appointment type, what is the logical next session? A follow-up call, review meeting, or implementation check-in?

2

Draft the Rebooking Message

Write a message that explains the value of the next session and includes your direct calendar booking link. Keep it concise and action-oriented.

3

Add to Workflow with One-Day Wait

In your workflow, add a wait step of approximately one day after the thank-you message, then add the rebooking invitation email or SMS.
