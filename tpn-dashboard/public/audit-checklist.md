## Common Leaks to Look For

| Leak | What You See | What It Costs |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| **Scattered tools** | Calendly + Mailchimp + Sheets + WordPress | Paying for 4+ tools, no sync, manual work |
| **No follow-up automation** | "Thanks for contacting us" only | 80% of leads go cold |
| **No lead capture** | Just contact form, no lead magnet | Visitors leave, no follow-up |
| **Manual booking** | "Call us to schedule" | Phone tag, lost appointments |
| **No CRM** | Tracking in spreadsheets | Forgotten leads, missed follow-ups |
| **Broken handoffs** | Website → Email → Phone → ??? | Leads fall through cracks |
| **No email nurture** | One welcome email, then silence | No relationship building |
| **Multiple payment tools** | Stripe + PayPal + Square | Double fees, reconciliation headaches |
